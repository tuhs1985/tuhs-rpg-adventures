**Appearance & First Impression**  
Elsera Venn carries herself with the quiet confidence of someone who keeps an entire riverfront running. Her clothing is practical—river-green sash, heavy boots, sleeves rolled up—but well-kept, hinting at a careful, organized nature. Silver-blonde hair is tied back to keep it clear of the wind coming off the Kyonin River, and her eyes track every movement on the docks the moment the party arrives. She speaks with a steady, businesslike tone, but not an unfriendly one.

---

**Personality & Mannerisms**  
Elsera is direct, efficient, and expects the same from others. She rarely wastes words. When thinking, she taps the corner of a small ledger with her thumb or glances downriver out of habit. Stress doesn’t make her loud—it makes her sharper, faster, and more focused. Despite her professional exterior, she shows faint amusement at small absurdities, usually with a dry comment weighed under her breath.

---

**Role in the Story**  
Elsera is the party’s anchor point for all things related to river travel, barge traffic, and the local economy. She provides information, work, and logistical support. She’s not an adventurer and doesn’t pretend to be one, but she understands the value—and danger—of the tasks she assigns. She can also serve as a recurring quest giver tied to trade routes, river hazards, or regional tensions.

---

**Background (Short)**  
Elsera grew up along the Kyonin River, apprenticed under the previous dockmaster before taking over the post. Years of dealing with bargemen, merchants, and riverfolk have given her a steady hand and a sharp instinct for trouble. She’s respected in Riverspire for keeping goods moving and avoiding bureaucratic headaches from the larger cities downstream.

---

**Goals & Motivations**  
- Keep the docks running cleanly and safely—her reputation rests on reliability.  
- Maintain good relationships with captains and merchants; trade is Riverspire’s lifeline.  
- Prevent accidents like the missing barge from becoming political or financial disasters.  
- Quietly hopes to improve the towpath infrastructure, though the council never approves the funding.

---

**Secrets & Complications**  
- She blames herself more than she shows for any lost crew or damaged vessel; she double-checks storm warnings obsessively since a near-disaster years ago.  
- Owes a private favor to a Lasinavel merchant house—harmless now, but it could complicate things if politics shift.  
- Has noticed subtle increases in river agitation and odd activity among wildlife but hasn’t spoken of it yet, not wanting to sound alarmist.

---

**Relationships**  
- **Riverspire Council:** Handles them professionally, but finds their debates tiresome.  
- **Local Bargemasters:** Good rapport; they trust her judgment on weather and water levels.  
- **Lasinavel Merchants:** Respect her efficiency but pressure her for faster turnaround times.  
- **River Wardens:** Cooperative, though she thinks they’re understaffed for the territory they patrol.

---

**Social & Mechanical Notes**  
- **Make an Impression (Diplomacy DC 17):** Responds best to clear, concise reasoning and demonstrated competence.  
- **Request (DC 18–20):** More amenable if the request helps protect river traffic or resolves outstanding risks.  
- **Lie to Her (Perception DC 19):** Keen at spotting half-truths; she knows when someone isn’t confident in their story.  
- **Assist or Gather Information:** Mentioning river conditions, trade concerns, or barges earns a +1 circumstance bonus.

---

**Tactics (If Combat Is Likely)**  
Elsera avoids fights entirely—she calls for help or retreats toward guards rather than engaging. If cornered, she uses improvised tools or anything heavy at hand but seeks to disengage as soon as possible.

---

**Rewards / Follow-Up Hooks**  
- Additional river-related work: escorting barges, locating missing crews, addressing towpath threats.  
- Updates on shifting trade patterns or political pressure from Lasinavel.  
- A growing sense of trust—she may eventually share concerns about strange river activity or ask for help with matters she can’t address publicly.
