# Rurik Granitevein
## Creature 1 (Prospector 3)

==Unique== ==Medium== ==Dwarf== <!-- Portrait -->![rurik-granitevein-prospector-dwarf.png|npc-portrait-right](/img/user/Assets/rurik-granitevein-prospector-dwarf.png)

**Source** NPC Core pg. __  

**Perception** +6  

**Languages** Common, Dwarven  

**Skills** Crafting +8, Diplomacy +6, Society +6, Survival +7, Mining Lore +9  

**Str** +2, **Dex** +0, **Con** +3, **Int** +1, **Wis** +2, **Cha** +1   

---

**Abilities** 

**Claim Sense**  
Rurik gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Crafting, Survival, and Mining Lore checks related to evaluating stone quality, ore potential, and excavation risk.

**Hard Bargainer**  
Rurik gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks made to negotiate contracts, payouts, or terms of employment.

**Risk Blindness**  
When presented with evidence that a project is dangerous but profitable, Rurik treats the result as one step more favorable than it actually is (failure as partial success, partial success as success).

**Items** Heavy pick, reinforced traveling clothes, surveying tools, ledger, sealed contract case, 15 gp  
 
---

**AC** 16; **Fort** +8, **Ref** +4, **Will** +6  

**HP** 22   

---

**Speed** 20 feet  

**Melee** `[one-action]` pick +7 (fatal d10), **Damage** 1d6+2 piercing  

**Ranged** `[one-action]` throwing hammer +5 (thrown 10 ft), **Damage** 1d6+2 bludgeoning  

---

**Appearance & First Impression**  
A broad-shouldered dwarf with stone-dust ground into his beard and cuffs. His clothes are practical but well-maintained, his boots recently resoled. He smells faintly of oil and old parchment. When he looks at rock, his eyes linger longer than they should.

---

**Personality & Mannerisms**  
Direct, impatient, and transactional. Rurik speaks plainly and expects the same in return. He listens closely when people talk about stone, danger, or cost—but not always to the conclusions they draw. When frustrated, he rubs his thumb along the spine of his ledger without realizing it.

---

**Role in the Story**  
Rurik is the expedition’s patron and financial driver. He provides contracts, payment, and pressure. His belief in the claim pushes the story forward even when evidence suggests caution.

---

**Background (Short)**  
Rurik Granitevein made his name reopening marginal claims others abandoned as unworkable. Enough of them paid off that he now trusts his instincts more than experts. He is wealthy, but most of that wealth is tied up in ongoing ventures, making each new claim feel necessary.

---

**Goals & Motivations**  
- Establish a profitable mine near the Kyonin border.  
- Secure written proof the site is “workable” to justify further investment.  
- Expand his influence among prospectors and investors without drawing political attention.

---

**Secrets & Complications**  
- Rurik has already promised partial rights to the claim to a silent backer.  
- He downplays or reframes danger reports when relaying information upward.  
- Walking away from this site would cost him more than he admits.

---

**Relationships**  
- **Investors:** Expect results; patience is thin.  
- **Local Authorities:** Tolerated, not trusted.  
- **Other Prospectors:** Mixture of resentment and grudging respect.

---

**Social & Mechanical Notes**  
- **Make an Impression (Diplomacy DC 16):** Clear, practical arguments work best.  
- **Request (DC 18):** More likely to agree if the request reduces cost or delay.  
- **Lie to Him (Perception DC 17):** He is used to being lied to, but tends to believe people who sound confident and prepared.

---

**Tactics (If Combat Is Likely)**  
Rurik avoids fights whenever possible. If forced, he stays behind tougher allies, uses terrain for cover, and disengages quickly. He will flee rather than risk serious injury.

---

**Rewards / Follow-Up Hooks**  
- Continued contracts and paid expeditions.  
- Pressure to reinterpret or “clarify” dangerous findings.  
- Potential future antagonist if his ambitions outpace the party’s willingness to enable them.
