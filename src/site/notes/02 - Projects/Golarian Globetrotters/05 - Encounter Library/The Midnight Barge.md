# The Midnight Barge
## Encounter 1 (Severe)

==Encounter== ==Severe== ==Riverspire== ==Undead== ==City Watch==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 110 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 5 gp

---

**Scenario**  
Shortly after midnight, a large river barge emerges from the fog and drifts silently toward Northbank Post. No lanterns burn aboard, no crew answer hails, and the vessel grounds itself against the dock with a heavy thud.

As the party investigates, movement can be heard below deck. Moments later, the dead begin climbing from the vessel's hold.

**Creatures**
- **[Weak Zombie Hound](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2041&Weak=true&NoRedirect=1)** ×2 (30 XP)
- **[Zombie Shambler](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3249&NoRedirect=1)** ×2 (40 XP)
- **[Plague Zombie](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3250&Redirected=1)** ×1 (40 XP)

**Reinforcements**
- Zombie Hounds appear when the vessel is investigated.
- Zombie Shamblers emerge from below deck at the start of Round 3.
- The Plague Zombie appears at the start of Round 5.

---

**Terrain & Features**
- **River Dock:** 10-foot-wide wooden dock. Falling into the river requires 1 action to Swim to safety.
- **Fog:** Creatures more than 30 feet away are Concealed.
- **Cargo Crates:** Provide standard cover.

---

**Tactics**
- The zombie hounds rush the nearest living creature and attempt to force opponents away from the vessel.
- The shamblers advance relentlessly toward the largest concentration of targets.
- The plague zombie remains below deck until released, then focuses on the nearest creature and presses the attack without regard for its own safety.
- None of the undead retreat.

---

**Development**
- The vessel contains no surviving crew.
- Waterlogged cargo and ruined paperwork suggest the barge has been adrift for some time.
- A damaged shipping manifest indicates the vessel originated somewhere far upriver, though its final destination is unreadable.

---

**Encounter Variants**
- **Harder:** Add 1 Weak Zombie Shambler to Wave 2.
- **Easier:** Remove 1 Weak Zombie Hound from Wave 1.
- **Environmental Variant:** Heavy rain reduces visibility to 20 feet and makes the dock difficult terrain.

---
