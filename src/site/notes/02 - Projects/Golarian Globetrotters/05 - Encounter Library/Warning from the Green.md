# Warning from the Green
## Encounter 2 (Severe)

==Encounter== ==Severe== ==Kyonin Borderlands==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 120 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---  
  
**Scenario**  
As the party approaches the abandoned mine, raised voices carry from the site. Workers shout warnings. Tools lie scattered near the entrance. Something moves between the trees just beyond the cleared ground.  
  
A shape of bark and thorn lunges from the treeline, driving laborers back toward the mine mouth. Smaller shapes skitter through brush and debris, striking at exposed workers.  
  
This is not a random attack.  
The site is being pushed back.  
  
---  
  
**Creatures**  
- **[Twigjack Weak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3222)**  
- **[Sprigjack](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3221)**  
  
(Optional scaling: add 1 additional Weak Twigjack if party is strong)  

---  
  
**Initiative**  
The creatures are already engaged with workers when the PCs arrive.  

- Workers act as environmental hazards unless protected  
  
---  
  
**Terrain & Features**  
- Mine entrance and rough scaffolding  
- Stacked lumber and supply crates (Cover)  
- Uneven ground, loose stone near the mine mouth  
- Workers in the area may need to be protected or directed  
- Treeline provides concealment to Fey creatures  
  
---  
  
**Tactics**  
- The Sprigjack focuses on driving workers away from the entrance  

---

**Special actions**

**Harass the Miners** `[two-actions]` (attack)

The fey makes a ranged Strike against a workers within 30 feet.

Attack: (of creature) vs AC 15

Critical Success: +2 Injuries.  
Success: +1 Injury.  
Failure: No effect.  
Critical Failure: The fey is Frightened 1.

- Every **2 Injuries = 1 Dead Worker**  
  
**Payout Impact**  
- 0–1 Dead: Full reward  
- 2–3 Dead: 50% reward  
- 4+ Dead: No reward
 
**Help the Miners** `[two-actions]`
Players may make a medicine check (DC 15) to remove an injury from a miner
 
---  
  
**Development**  
  
**Success:**  
Creatures are driven off. Workers shaken but alive. Site remains usable.  
  
**Partial:**  
Fey retreat but workers are injured or supplies damaged. Morale drops.  
  
**Failure:**  
Workers flee. Equipment damaged. Mine entrance temporarily unsafe.  
  
---  
  
**Encounter Variants**  
  
**Harder Variant:**  
- Increase battlefield control: brambles create Difficult Terrain in 2–3 squares per round  
- Add one additional Weak Twigjack  
  
**Easier Variant:**  
- Remove one Twigjack  
- Workers automatically disengage safely after 1 round  
  
**Environmental Variant:**  
- A worker is grabbed or cornered at the start; rescuing them becomes an immediate objective

---
