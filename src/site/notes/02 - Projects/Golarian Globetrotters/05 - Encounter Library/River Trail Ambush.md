# River Trail Ambush
## Encounter 1 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Riverbanks==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
Following the flooded towpath north of Riverspire, the heroes trace signs of the missing barge. The trail narrows where a pine has fallen across the bank, forming a makeshift bridge. Mud and river spray have turned the slope slick, and the roar of rushing water drowns out all but the nearest voices.  
As the group approaches the fallen tree, movement in the reeds reveals an ambush—boggards lying in wait with a trained giant frog, ready to drag intruders toward the river’s edge.

---

**Creatures**  
- **[Boggard Guard](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Guard&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4006)** ×2  
- **[Giant Frog](obsidian://opengate?title=Giant%20Frog&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4008)** ×1  
*(Adjust as needed to maintain ~80 XP total.)*

---

**Terrain & Features**  
- **Narrow Towpath:** 10 ft wide, bordered by steep 10 ft drop to the river.  
- **Slick Footing:** Striding more than 10 ft on the slope requires **Reflex DC 15** or fall prone.  
- **River Current:** Falling creatures are carried 10 ft downstream each round until they succeed a **Swim DC 13** check.

---

**Tactics**  
- Boggards start hidden behind the fallen pine, using **Stealth DC 16**.  
- They attack when half the party crosses the log, using **Tongue** to pull targets toward the water.  
- The giant frog joins after the first round, focusing on isolated PCs.

---

**Victory Conditions**  
- Ambushers routed or captured; clues on the bodies point upriver to the wreck site.
