# Night Eyes in the Green
## Encounter 1 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Borderlands==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
As camp settles during the journey, movement stirs in the dark. Shapes circle beyond the firelight — testing, then striking.

---

**Creatures**  
- 2x **[Wolf](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3241&Redirected=1)** (Level 1)  

---

**Initiative**  
The wolves stalk first and strike when they think they have advantage.

- Primary Initiative: **Stealth +7** (they begin Hidden, circling the camp)
- If a PC successfully detects them prior to the attack → **Perception +5**
- PCs on watch gain a +2 circumstance bonus to detect them, if that’s narratively justified

---

**Terrain & Features**  
- Patchy, shifting **dim light and firelight**
- **Natural cover** from rocks / scrub
- Uneven ground, scattered **camp gear as obstacles**
- Sleeping PCs require decisions: armor, readiness, movement, awareness

---

**Tactics**  
- Wolves harry and test the line first instead of rushing in
- Attempt to isolate a straggler; threaten but avoid open melee initially
- Become vicious if they draw blood (focus one target)
- Retreat or break off once both are moderately injured

---

**Development**  

**Success:** Predators retreat; camp remains intact  
**Partial:** Wolves driven off but party injured / rattled  
**Failure:** Supplies damaged, morale and rest disrupted

---

**Encounter Variants**  

**Harder Variant:**  
- Add **+1 Wolf** **OR**  
- A **Dire Wolf (Level 3)** enters from the treeline in Round 2

**Easier Variant:**  
- Reduce to **1 Wolf + 1 Level 0 animal**
- Remove flanking behavior; direct lunges only

**Environmental Variant:**  
- Rain or slick stone increases footing difficulty  
  - Acrobatics DCs **+2** when moving quickly
