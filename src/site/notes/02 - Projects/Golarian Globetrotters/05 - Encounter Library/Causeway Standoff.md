# Causeway Standoff
## Encounter 2 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Causeway==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
The heroes follow the river north until they reach a crumbling elven causeway spanning the Kyonic River. Shattered pillars and broken stones rise above the rapids. Atop the ruin, boggard raiders are stripping cargo from the recovered barge and holding its surviving crew hostage.

Three surviving **Riverspire deckhands**—bruised and bound—are forced to assist the raiders, moving crates toward the far side of the bridge. Their lives hang in the balance once the fighting starts.

---

**Creatures**  
- **[Boggard Scout](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Scout&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4000)** ×1 
- **[Boggard Guard](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Guard&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4006)** ×2  
*(Adjust numbers as needed to maintain ~80 XP total.)*

---

**Terrain**  
- **Ancient Elven Causeway:** 10 ft above rushing water; falling deals **1d6 bludgeoning** and sweeps a creature 20 ft downstream each round until they Swim DC 14 to recover.  
- **Broken Parapets:** Provide standard cover.  
- **Oil-Slick Crates:** Several leaking barrels create flammable hazards (see *Oil Fire Slick* hazard).  

---

**Tactics**  
- The leader positions the crew as living shields, forcing the PCs to choose between cautious rescue or direct assault.  Crew provides "Lesser Cover" for the Boggards. Critical miss with a range weapon strikes a crew member.  
- Boggard guards attempt to shove PCs toward the river; the leader focuses on holding hostages or setting the oil aflame if losing.

---

**Rescuing the Crew**  
- **Athletics DC 14** to cut bonds quickly.  
- **Diplomacy DC 15** to give freed crew simple commands

---

**Victory Conditions**  
- Defeat or rout the raiders (XP as normal).  
- Recover remaining cargo to complete the contract from Riverspire.

---

**Outcome**  
- **Victory:** Crew rescued, raiders defeated, cargo secured. The party earns full 80 XP.  
- **Partial:** Cargo burns; full XP, Gold reward reduced by half.  
- **Failure:** Raiders escape with captives; half XP (40).

# Oil Fire Slick
## Complex Hazard -1

==Hazard== ==Complex== ==Environmental== ==Fire==

**Stealth** +7 (trained) to notice the slicks before ignition; **Description** Leaking lamp oil has spread across the broken causeway stones and into shallow eddies below. Any spark or flame can ignite the film, sending fire racing across the surface.

---

**Disable** `[one-action]` **Crafting, Athletics, or Arcana DC 15** to smother, divert, or magically dampen a 5-foot patch of oil. Each successful check removes that section from the hazard’s area. The hazard ends once all patches are disabled or burn out.

**AC** 15 **Fort +5 Ref +8** **Hardness** 5 **HP** 20 (BT 10) per 5-foot patch

**Immunities** Critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

---

**Trigger**  
Any open flame, lightning effect, or critical hit that produces sparks within 10 feet of the slick ignites a patch.

---

**Routine** (1 action)  
At the start of each round after ignition, flames spread 5 feet in one direction (GM’s choice or by wind/current).  
Creatures in or adjacent to a burning patch take **1d4 fire damage (Basic Reflex DC 16)**.  
A creature that fails this save also catches **persistent 1 fire**.  
The slick burns for **2 rounds** after ignition, then gutters out unless reignited by another patch.

---

**Reset**  
The oil burns out naturally after 2 rounds of inactivity or if fully smothered.  
If new oil leaks from barrels, the hazard reforms in 10 minutes.

---

**Notes**  
- Treat each patch as a 5-foot hazard space; 4–6 patches typically cover the causeway.
