# Stones Beneath the Green
## Adventure Overview
==Adventure== ==Level 1==
**Party Level** 1 **Total XP Award** 160 **Total Treasure (Gold Only)** 8 gp

---

**Hooks**
- A wealthy dwarven prospector believes he has identified a promising mining claim near the Kyonin border.
- He needs capable adventurers to travel to the site, evaluate its viability, and establish a foothold camp.
- Rumors suggest Kyonin land reacts poorly to intrusion, but the money spends well.

---

**Scenario**  
A dwarven prospector has identified an **abandoned mining site** along the Kyonin frontier that may be worth reclaiming. The party is hired to travel to the site, evaluate whether the mine can be safely reopened, and establish a temporary camp while the assessment is conducted.

The mine was left idle years ago for reasons that were never properly documented. Whatever drove the original workers away, the land has not forgotten their presence.

---

**Scenes**
1. **Assess the Claim** — Victory Point Subsystem (80 XP)
2. **Night Eyes in the Green** — Moderate Combat (80 XP)


---

**Objectives**
- Travel into the borderlands and survive the journey.
- Assess whether the abandoned mine can be safely reclaimed and worked again.
- Establish a foothold and withstand the land’s response.

---

**Notable NPCs**
- [[02 - Projects/Golarian Globetrotters/01 - NPCs/Rurik Granitevein\|Rurik Granitevein]] — wealthy, driven, eager, desperate to legitimize his find.
- **The Fey Guardian** — a thinking, territorial embodiment of the wilderness responding to intrusion.

---

**Posting on the Job Board**  
> Looking for capable hands to evaluate a potential mining claim along the Kyonin frontier. Travel compensation and performance pay available. Expect wilderness danger. Need professionals, not loudmouthed heroes. Payment upon completion — do not waste my coin or my time.

---

**Adventure Scope**
- **Encounters:** 2 Combat, 1 Subsystem
- **Total XP:** 280 XP
- **Treasure Budget:** 18 gp (prospector payout; no wilderness loot expected)
- **Expected Duration:** 1 session (3–4 hours)

---

**Outcome Summary**
- **Success:**  
  The mine appears promising, the prospector is pleased, and the region remains dangerous but stable.

- **Partial Success:**  
  The mine appears usable but risky. The prospector proceeds, but the region becomes more dangerous going forward.

- **Failure:**  
  The assessment is muddled or troubling, morale and trust are shaken, and the region is now significantly more volatile.

---

**Rewards & Follow-Ups**
- Gold payout based on assessment success.
- Regional Random Encounter DC adjustments for future sessions.
- Establishes site as a future expedition hub.
- Introduces the Fey as an active, remembering force in the campaign.

# Assess the Claim
## Victory Point Subsystem (Moderate)

==Subsystem== ==Victory Point== ==Moderate==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
The party must evaluate an **abandoned mine site** to determine whether it can be safely reopened and expanded into a viable operation.. This is a full day of technical assessment in harsh wilderness conditions, where mistakes cause real physical and structural consequences.

---

**Subsystem Type & Structure**  
This is a Victory Point subsystem representing an 8-hour workday of evaluation.

- **Total Duration:** 8 hours of work  
- **Structure:** 4 assessment passes (each representing 2 hours)  
- **Each Pass:** One subsystem roll outcome  
- **Target:** 8 or more Victory Points for full success  

---

**Degrees of Success**  

- **Critical Success:** +2 Victory Points  
- **Success:** +1 Victory Point  
- **Failure:** +0 Victory Points; trigger a **Site Mishap (d10)**  
- **Critical Failure:** –1 Victory Point; trigger a **Site Mishap (d10)** and the acting PC gains **Shaken Confidence**  
  - –1 circumstance to their future checks in this subsystem  
  - –1 circumstance to Reflex saves against site mishaps  
  Shaken Confidence lasts until the workday ends or the PC rests.

---

**Primary Skills (Typical DC 15)**  

- **Crafting / Engineering Lore / Dwarven Lore:** Judge stone integrity, fault risk, and vein promise.  
- **Survival:** Evaluate environmental threats, exposure, and how workable a camp will be.  
- **Nature:** Read terrain strain, erosion risk, and ecological instability.  
- **Perception:** Spot cracks, subtle vibration, shifting stone, and hidden weak points.

---

**Secondary Skills (List limitations if any, DC __)**  

None. This subsystem intentionally has **no secondary skills**; all meaningful work uses primary skills.

---

**Consequences / Setbacks**  
Failures and critical failures do not simply stall progress; they injure workers, strain morale, break tools, and destabilize the site. Each mishap represents the land and stone pushing back.

Whenever a roll calls for a Site Mishap, roll **1d10** and apply the result:

- **1–2 — Falling Stone:** Loose rock shifts overhead; 1d6 bludgeoning (DC 15 Basic Reflex).  
- **3–4 — Exhausting Labor:** One PC becomes **Fatigued** until they rest.  
- **5 — Shock Through the Line:** Weight shifts suddenly; all participating PCs attempt **DC 15 Fortitude**. On a failure, they become **Clumsy 1** until rest.  
- **6 — Tool Failure:** A critical brace, rope, or tool breaks. The party takes a **–1 circumstance penalty to all future checks** in this subsystem until repairs or the day ends.  
- **7 — Hard Hit:** A brace slips or the stone bites back; one PC takes **1d8 bludgeoning** damage.  
- **8 — Confidence Fracture:** Doubt spreads. Until the group earns **at least 1 VP**, everyone suffers **–1 morale** to their checks in this subsystem.  
- **9 — Choking Dust Bloom:** Fine stone dust fills the air; all participating PCs attempt **DC 15 Fortitude**. On a failure, they become **Sickened 1** and take **–1 Perception** for the remainder of the assessment.  
- **10 — Compromised Ground + Escalation:** The terrain is destabilized in a lasting way. All site-based hazards here gain **+2 DC**, and the next encounter at this site grants enemies **+2 circumstance to Initiative**. Then roll again on this table and apply the new result as well.

---

**Outcomes**  

After four assessment passes (8 hours total), total the Victory Points:

- **8+ Victory Points — Success:**  
  - The site appears viable and promising.  
  - **Payout:** Full reward, 18 gp total (distributed per your server rules).  
  - **Future Random Encounters:** The regional Random Encounter flat check DC remains **15**.

- **5–7 Victory Points — Partial Success:**  
  - The site appears workable, but clear risks and instability remain. The prospector proceeds with caution.  
  - **Payout:** Half reward, 9 gp total.  
  - **Future Random Encounters:** The region becomes more dangerous; **Random Encounter flat check DC becomes 13** (encounters more likely).

- **0–4 Victory Points — Failure:**  
  - The assessment is inconclusive or troubling, yet the prospector may still push ahead out of stubbornness or sunk cost.  
  - **Payout:** Half reward, 9 gp total, paid grudgingly.  
  - **Future Random Encounters:** The region is now volatile and hazardous; **Random Encounter flat check DC becomes 10** (encounters significantly more likely).

# Camp Preparation — Site Defense Readiness
## Short Subsystem

==Subsystem== ==Camp== ==Preparation==

**Party Level** — Variable **XP** — None **Treasure** — None

---

**Scenario**  
Before nightfall, the party prepares a defensible camp near the prospective claim. This subsystem represents how well they secure the area, organize rest, and stabilize morale. It does **not** change how likely an encounter is; it determines how ready the party is if something attacks during the night.

---

**Subsystem Type & Structure**  

This is a lightweight preparation subsystem that modifies the next night encounter at this camp.

- **Prep Window:** Up to 3 hours before sleep.  
- Each PC may take **one meaningful prep action**.  
- Each action is **one Skill Check** tied to a prep category.  
- **Prep Points:** Success and critical success grant Prep Points in that category.  
- **Cap:** Maximum of 6 total Prep Points across all categories to avoid runaway stacking.

**Degrees of success for individual prep checks:**

- **Critical Success:** Gain **2 Prep Points** in the chosen category.  
- **Success:** Gain **1 Prep Point** in the chosen category.  
- **Failure:** No effect.  
- **Critical Failure:** Minor mishap (such as 1d6 damage or starting the encounter **Frightened 1**, GM’s choice based on fiction).

---

**Degrees of Success**  

The subsystem itself does not have a pass/fail VP total; instead, the total Prep Points across categories determine modifiers applied if a night encounter occurs.

- Higher total points → stronger opening position, better initiative, more durability.  
- Poor prep or multiple critical failures → weaker start, more exposed camp.

---

**Primary Skills (Typical DC 15)**  

The exact skill depends on the chosen prep category:

- **Fortify the Camp (Physical Defense):**  
  - Crafting, Athletics, Survival, Engineering Lore, Dwarven Lore  
  - Building barricades, trenches, bracing stone, shaping chokepoints.

- **Establish Watch & Early Warning (Awareness):**  
  - Perception, Survival, Stealth  
  - Setting lookouts, noise traps, alarm lines, and sentry patterns.

- **Secure Living Space (Morale & Stability):**  
  - Survival, Medicine, Diplomacy, Performance  
  - Organizing shelters, reinforcing rest, tending stress and minor wounds.

- **Appease / Respect the Wild (Optional, Thematic):**  
  - Nature, Religion, Diplomacy, Occultism  
  - Offerings, ritual gestures, symbolic respect to the land and its spirits.  
  - Recommended DC 17 (fey and spirits are fickle).

---

**Secondary Skills (List limitations if any, DC __)**  

None defined. All meaningful contributions use the primary skills above in the context of a chosen prep category.

---

**Consequences / Setbacks**  

Prep failures represent wasted effort, awkward layout, or frayed nerves. Critical failures represent small injuries, embarrassment, or stress that may carry into the encounter (GM chooses an appropriate minor penalty: 1d6 damage, Frightened 1, or similar).

Prep does **not** prevent an encounter from happening. Instead, it determines:

- Who is ready and in position  
- Who has better initiative  
- Who has better cover or defense  
- Whether the enemy hesitates or hits immediately

---

**Outcomes**  

If **no night encounter occurs**, the subsystem simply reinforces that the party took their work seriously; no mechanical effect is required.

If a **night encounter occurs at this camp**, apply:

- **Fortify the Camp (Fortification Points)**  
  - Each Fortification Point: one PC gains **+1 AC vs their first attack**.  
  - At **3+ Fortification Points:** the camp clearly has established cover; PCs may claim Cover from built defenses without argument.

- **Establish Watch & Early Warning (Awareness Points)**  
  - Each Awareness Point: assign **+1 Initiative** to a PC.  
  - At **2+ Awareness Points:** the party is **not Flat-Footed** at the start of the encounter.  
  - At **3+ Awareness Points:** they detect the threat moments earlier in narration, reinforcing their readiness.

- **Secure Living Space (Morale Points)**  
  - Each Morale Point: all PCs gain **1 Temporary HP** at the start of the encounter.  
  - At **2+ Morale Points:** +1 bonus to the **first Will save** each PC attempts in the encounter.  
  - At **3+ Morale Points:** remove **Fatigued** conditions gained from the site work earlier that day.

- **Appease / Respect the Wild (Ritual Prep)**  
  - On **Success (DC 17):** the first hostile attack against the party deals **–1 damage**.  
  - On **Critical Success:** the enemy hesitates; they **lose their first Reaction** in round 1.  
  - On **Critical Failure:** the attempt offends the land or its spirits; the enemy gains **+1 to their first attack roll**.

These effects apply only to the **next night encounter at this camp** and do not persist beyond it.

# Night Eyes in the Green
## Encounter 1 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Borderlands==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
As camp settles during the journey, movement stirs in the dark. Shapes circle beyond the firelight — testing, then striking.

---

**Creatures**  
- 2x **[Wolf](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3241&Redirected=1)** (Level 1)  

---

**Initiative**  
The wolves stalk first and strike when they think they have advantage.

- Primary Initiative: **Stealth +7** (they begin Hidden, circling the camp)
- If a PC successfully detects them prior to the attack → **Perception +5**
- PCs on watch gain a +2 circumstance bonus to detect them, if that’s narratively justified

---

**Terrain & Features**  
- Patchy, shifting **dim light and firelight**
- **Natural cover** from rocks / scrub
- Uneven ground, scattered **camp gear as obstacles**
- Sleeping PCs require decisions: armor, readiness, movement, awareness

---

**Tactics**  
- Wolves harry and test the line first instead of rushing in
- Attempt to isolate a straggler; threaten but avoid open melee initially
- Become vicious if they draw blood (focus one target)
- Retreat or break off once both are moderately injured

---

**Development**  

**Success:** Predators retreat; camp remains intact  
**Partial:** Wolves driven off but party injured / rattled  
**Failure:** Supplies damaged, morale and rest disrupted

---

**Encounter Variants**  

**Harder Variant:**  
- Add **+1 Wolf** **OR**  
- A **Dire Wolf (Level 3)** enters from the treeline in Round 2

**Easier Variant:**  
- Reduce to **1 Wolf + 1 Level 0 animal**
- Remove flanking behavior; direct lunges only

**Environmental Variant:**  
- Rain or slick stone increases footing difficulty  
  - Acrobatics DCs **+2** when moving quickly
