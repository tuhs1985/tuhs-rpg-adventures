# Bound in Iron
## Adventure Overview
==Adventure== ==Level 1== ==Kyonin Boarderlands==
**Party Level** 1 **Total XP Award** 220 **Total Treasure (Gold Only)** 10 gp

---

**Hooks**
- Reports of attacks at the mine site.  
- Workers injured; operations halted.  
- Rurik Granitevein requests immediate stabilization.

---

**Scenario**  
After crews are sent to begin reclaiming the abandoned mine, disturbances escalate into open violence. When the party arrives, fey creatures are actively attacking workers near the entrance. Once the threat is driven off, the site must be secured with a structured dwarven ward to prevent continued incursions. The ward will hold — but something beneath the mine stirs when it is established.


---

**Scenes**
1. **Warning from the Green (Worksite Assault)** — Severe Combat (120 XP)  
2. **Warding the Claim** — Victory Point Subsystem (100 XP total)  
- Mid-ritual Disruption Encounter (80 XP worth of creatures)

---

**Objectives**
- Protect workers during the attack.  
- Minimize casualties.  
- Establish a functioning ward at the mine entrance.

---

**Notable NPCs**
- **Rurik Granitevein** — Patron; pressing for stability and continued operation.  
- **Mine Foreman (TBD)** — Represents worker morale and practical concerns. 

---

**Posting on the Job Board**  
> Immediate assistance required at the Granitevein claim. Workers report violent resistance at the mine site. Stabilization and perimeter security required. Compensation adjusted based on outcome.

---

**Adventure Scope**
- **Encounters:** 1 Severe Combat, 1 Subsystem, 1 Moderate Combat (mid-ritual)  
- **Total XP:** 220 XP  
- **Treasure Budget:** Determined by worker casualties  
- **Expected Duration:** 1 Session (3–4 hours)

---

**Outcome Summary**
- **Low Casualties / Stable Ward:** Work resumes; confidence restored.  
- **Moderate Casualties / Strained Ward:** Operations continue cautiously; tension remains.  
- **Heavy Casualties:** Workers reluctant; future reinforcement missions likely.

---

**Rewards & Follow-Ups**
- Payment reduced if 2+ workers die.  
- The ward disturbs something beneath the mine.  
- Session 3: Reports of underground instability and renewed danger.

# Warning from the Green
## Encounter 2 (Severe)

==Encounter== ==Severe== ==Kyonin Borderlands==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 120 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---  
  
**Scenario**  
As the party approaches the abandoned mine, raised voices carry from the site. Workers shout warnings. Tools lie scattered near the entrance. Something moves between the trees just beyond the cleared ground.  
  
A shape of bark and thorn lunges from the treeline, driving laborers back toward the mine mouth. Smaller shapes skitter through brush and debris, striking at exposed workers.  
  
This is not a random attack.  
The site is being pushed back.  
  
---  
  
**Creatures**  
- **[Twigjack Weak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3222)**  
- **[Sprigjack](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3221)**  
  
(Optional scaling: add 1 additional Weak Twigjack if party is strong)  

---  
  
**Initiative**  
The creatures are already engaged with workers when the PCs arrive.  

- Workers act as environmental hazards unless protected  
  
---  
  
**Terrain & Features**  
- Mine entrance and rough scaffolding  
- Stacked lumber and supply crates (Cover)  
- Uneven ground, loose stone near the mine mouth  
- Workers in the area may need to be protected or directed  
- Treeline provides concealment to Fey creatures  
  
---  
  
**Tactics**  
- The Sprigjack focuses on driving workers away from the entrance  

---

**Special actions**

**Harass the Miners** `[two-actions]` (attack)

The fey makes a ranged Strike against a workers within 30 feet.

Attack: (of creature) vs AC 15

Critical Success: +2 Injuries.  
Success: +1 Injury.  
Failure: No effect.  
Critical Failure: The fey is Frightened 1.

- Every **2 Injuries = 1 Dead Worker**  
  
**Payout Impact**  
- 0–1 Dead: Full reward  
- 2–3 Dead: 50% reward  
- 4+ Dead: No reward
  
---  
  
**Development**  
  
**Success:**  
Creatures are driven off. Workers shaken but alive. Site remains usable.  
  
**Partial:**  
Fey retreat but workers are injured or supplies damaged. Morale drops.  
  
**Failure:**  
Workers flee. Equipment damaged. Mine entrance temporarily unsafe.  
  
---  
  
**Encounter Variants**  
  
**Harder Variant:**  
- Increase battlefield control: brambles create Difficult Terrain in 2–3 squares per round  
- Add one additional Weak Twigjack  
  
**Easier Variant:**  
- Remove one Twigjack  
- Workers automatically disengage safely after 1 round  
  
**Environmental Variant:**  
- A worker is grabbed or cornered at the start; rescuing them becomes an immediate objective

# Warding the Claim
## Victory Point Subsystem (Moderate)

==Subsystem== ==Victory Point== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Borderlands==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 100 **Treasure (Gold Only)**  4.38gp

---

**Scenario**  
Following the attack at the mine, the site must be stabilized. A dwarven reclamation ward is established around the entrance: iron stakes driven into the perimeter, a boundary line marked, and a rune anchor carved into the stone lintel. The ward will go up — but the land does not yield quietly.

---

**Subsystem Type & Structure**  

- **Critical Success:** +2 VP  
- **Success:** +1 VP  
- **Failure:** +0 VP and apply skill-based backlash  
- **Critical Failure:** +0 VP, apply skill-based backlash and one adjacent PC becomes Off-Guard (1 round) 

---

**Degrees of Success**  
*List the outcomes for Crit Success, Success, Failure, Crit Failure.*  

Example (Victory Point Model):  
- **Critical Success:** +2 VP  
- **Success:** +1 VP  
- **Failure:** No VP; +1 setback or environmental penalty  
- **Critical Failure:** –1 VP; +1 setback; hazardous effect triggers (Basic Reflex DC 15)

---

**Primary Skills (Typical DC 15)**  
*Skills the PCs are expected to use repeatedly.*  

- **Crafting:** Inscribe and secure ward-stakes  
- **Athletics:** Drive iron stakes deep into the soil  
- **Arcana:** Align the ward’s geometric pattern  
- **Religion:** Invoke lawful boundary over wild influence  
- **Survival:** Position perimeter markers correctly

---

**Consequences / Setbacks**  
*Describe what failures represent narratively.*  

**Backlash by Skill (1 Round Duration)**  
  
- Crafting → Clumsy 1  
- Athletics → Enfeebled 1  
- Arcana → Off-Guard  
- Religion → Frightened 1  
- Survival → Off-Guard
  
  If no ritual action is taken in a round:  
- All PCs within 20 ft of the anchor become Off-Guard (1 round)

---  
  
**Disruption Event (At 4 VP)**  
  
The ward strikes resistance. Stakes shudder. The rune dims. Roots tear through disturbed soil.  
  
Spawn creatures totaling **80 XP** (Moderate encounter).  
  
The ritual continues during combat.  
If a full round passes with no ritual check:  
- The ward destabilizes (Off-Guard pulse as above)
  
---

**Outcomes**  
*(Flavor Only)*  
  
- **6–8 VP:** Ward steady; minor tremor below  
- **3–5 VP:** Ward stable but strained; workers uneasy  
- **0–2 VP:** Ward stands; noticeable tremor from deeper stone  
  
The ward holds in all cases.
