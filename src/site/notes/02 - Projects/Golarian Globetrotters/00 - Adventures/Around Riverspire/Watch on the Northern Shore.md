# Watch on the Northern Shore
## Adventure Overview
==Adventure== ==Level 1== ==Riverspire== ==City Watch== ==Undead==
**Party Level** 1 **Total XP Award** 80 **Total Treasure (Gold Only)** x gp

--- 

**Hooks** 
- The Riverside Watch seeks temporary auxiliaries to man Northbank Post for a single night. 
- Several guards have recently refused assignments to the isolated station. 
- A generous shift bonus is being offered for anyone willing to complete the watch.

---  
  
**Scenario**  
The PCs accept an overnight watch contract at Northbank Post, a lonely guard station north of Riverspire overlooking the Kyonin River. 

The assignment appears uneventful until shortly after midnight when a river barge emerges from the fog. The vessel drifts without sail, crew, or lanterns, eventually grounding itself near the post's dock. 

Investigation reveals the barge is carrying far more than abandoned cargo.

---  
  
**Scenes**  
1. **Northbank Post** — Exploration  
Inspect the watch station, read previous watch logs, and prepare for the night.  
  
2. **The River Delivers** — Exploration  
Thick fog settles over the water. An unmanned barge appears and grounds near the dock.  
  
3. **The Dead Aboard** — Combat, 110 XP  
Wave encounter aboard the barge and around the dock.  
- Wave 1: 2 Weak Zombie Hounds (30 XP)   
- Wave 2: 2 Zombie Shamblers (40 XP) 
- Wave 3: 1 Plague Zombie (40 XP)

---  
  
**Objectives**  
- Investigate the drifting barge.  
- Survive the undead attack.  
- Recover evidence explaining the vessel's origin.

---

**Notable NPCs**  
None. The contract is issued through the Riverspire Watch office.

---

**Posting on the Job Board**  
> **CITY WATCH AUXILIARIES NEEDED**  
>  
> Northbank Post requires temporary auxiliaries for a routine overnight assignment.  
>  
> Duties include monitoring river traffic, maintaining the signal brazier, and reporting suspicious activity.  
>  
>  
> Shift begins at dusk. Payment upon return.

---  
  
**Adventure Scope**  
- **Encounters:** 2 exploration scenes, 1 combat wave encounter  
- **Total XP:** 110 XP  
- **Treasure Budget:** x gp  
- **Expected Duration:** 2–3 hours

---  
  
**Outcome Summary**  
- **Success:** The party destroys the undead and recovers evidence from the vessel.  
- **Partial Success:** The undead are defeated but key evidence is lost or destroyed.  
- **Failure:** The party retreats or is overwhelmed, allowing the vessel and its cargo to continue downstream.
  
---

**Rewards & Follow-Ups**  
Recovered documents indicate the vessel originated far upriver, though portions of the manifest have been destroyed by water damage.  
  
Among the cargo is an old religious symbol recognized as originating from settlements near the Isle of Terror.  
  
While no immediate expedition is planned, local authorities quietly begin collecting reports of unusual activity along the river.

# The Midnight Barge
## Encounter 1 (Severe)

==Encounter== ==Severe== ==Riverspire== ==Undead== ==City Watch==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 110 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 5 gp

---

**Scenario**  
Shortly after midnight, a large river barge emerges from the fog and drifts silently toward Northbank Post. No lanterns burn aboard, no crew answer hails, and the vessel grounds itself against the dock with a heavy thud.

As the party investigates, movement can be heard below deck. Moments later, the dead begin climbing from the vessel's hold.

**Creatures**
- **[Weak Zombie Hound](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2041&Weak=true&NoRedirect=1)** ×2 (30 XP)
- **[Zombie Shambler](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3249&NoRedirect=1)** ×2 (40 XP)
- **[Plague Zombie](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3250&Redirected=1)** ×1 (40 XP)

**Reinforcements**
- Zombie Hounds appear when the vessel is investigated.
- Zombie Shamblers emerge from below deck at the start of Round 3.
- The Plague Zombie appears at the start of Round 5.

---

**Initiative**
- Creatures use **Perception** for initiative.
- Wave 1 appears once the party boards the vessel or begins searching the deck.
- Wave 2 emerges from below deck at the start of Round 3.
- Wave 3 breaks through a locked hatch or cargo partition at the start of Round 5.

---

**Terrain & Features**
- **River Dock:** 10-foot-wide wooden dock. Falling into the river requires 1 action to Swim to safety.
- **Fog:** Creatures more than 30 feet away are Concealed.
- **Gangplank:** Narrow approach to the vessel; creatures cannot move through occupied spaces.
- **Cargo Crates:** Provide standard cover.
- **Below Deck:** Tight quarters with narrow passages that limit movement and lines of sight.

---

**Tactics**
- The zombie hounds rush the nearest living creature and attempt to force opponents off the gangplank or away from the vessel.
- The shamblers advance relentlessly toward the largest concentration of targets.
- The plague zombie remains below deck until released, then focuses on the nearest creature and presses the attack without regard for its own safety.
- None of the undead retreat.

---

**Development**
- The vessel contains no surviving crew.
- Waterlogged cargo and ruined paperwork suggest the barge has been adrift for some time.
- A damaged shipping manifest indicates the vessel originated somewhere far upriver, though its final destination is unreadable.
- News of the attack spreads through Riverspire, lending credibility to rumors of unusual activity along the river.

---

**Encounter Variants**
- **Harder:** Add 1 Weak Zombie Shambler to Wave 2.
- **Easier:** Remove 1 Weak Zombie Hound from Wave 1.
- **Environmental Variant:** Heavy rain reduces visibility to 20 feet and makes the dock difficult terrain.

---
