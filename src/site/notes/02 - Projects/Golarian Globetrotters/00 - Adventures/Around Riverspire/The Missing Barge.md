# The Missing Barge
## Adventure Overview
==Adventure== ==Level 1== ==Kyonin River== 
**Party Level** 1 **Total XP Award** 240 **Total Treasure (Gold Only)** 11 gp

---

**Scenario**  
As autumn sets in across Kyonin, the river runs high from seasonal rains, its waters cold and fast beneath fading leaves. A Riverspire trade barge bound for Lasinavel vanished after the most recent storm, leaving dockworkers whispering about raiders and wreckage seen upriver.  
The dockmaster, **[[02 - Projects/Golarian Globetrotters/01 - NPCs/Elsera Venn\|Elsera Venn]]**, now seeks capable hands to recover the missing cargo before Lasinavel’s merchant guild imposes costly fines. Rumors from river scouts speak of strange shapes moving near the old elven causeway—perhaps scavengers drawn by the wreck, or something more deliberate.

This short adventure spans the river route between **Riverspire** and **Lasinavel**, divided into three connected scenes:  
1. **River Trail Ambush** — Combat encounter (Moderate 80 XP)  
2. **Wrecked Barge Stabilization** — Victory Point subsystem (Moderate 80 XP)  
3. **Old Causeway Standoff** — Combat encounter (Moderate 80 XP)

---

**Posting on the Job Board**  
> *“River shipment overdue.  Last seen rounding the South Fork bend after the storm.  Three crew missing.  Reward: 4 gold per hand recovered, plus salvage rights on damaged goods.”*  
> — Elsera Venn, Dockmaster of Riverspire

---

**Objectives**  
- Locate and stabilize the missing barge.  
- Rescue any surviving crew and secure its cargo.  
- Return proof of recovery to Riverspire before fines are imposed.

---

**Adventure Scope**  
- **Encounters:** 2 Combat, 1 Skill Subsystem  
- **Total XP:** 240 XP (3 Moderate encounters)  
- **Treasure Budget:** 54 gp (18 gp per encounter)  
- **Expected Duration:** 1 session / 3–4 hours

---

**Outcome Summary**  
- **Success:** The party recovers the barge and crew, preserving Riverspire’s trade reputation.  
- **Partial Success:** The barge is lost or damaged, but the crew survive.  
- **Failure:** Raiders escape with cargo or captives, creating hooks for a follow-up mission.

# River Trail Ambush
## Encounter 1 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Riverbanks==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
Following the flooded towpath north of Riverspire, the heroes trace signs of the missing barge. The trail narrows where a pine has fallen across the bank, forming a makeshift bridge. Mud and river spray have turned the slope slick, and the roar of rushing water drowns out all but the nearest voices.  
As the group approaches the fallen tree, movement in the reeds reveals an ambush—boggards lying in wait with a trained giant frog, ready to drag intruders toward the river’s edge.

---

**Creatures**  
- **[Boggard Guard](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Guard&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4006)** ×2  
- **[Giant Frog](obsidian://opengate?title=Giant%20Frog&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4008)** ×1  
*(Adjust as needed to maintain ~80 XP total.)*

---

**Terrain & Features**  
- **Narrow Towpath:** 10 ft wide, bordered by steep 10 ft drop to the river.  
- **Slick Footing:** Striding more than 10 ft on the slope requires **Reflex DC 15** or fall prone.  
- **River Current:** Falling creatures are carried 10 ft downstream each round until they succeed a **Swim DC 13** check.

---

**Tactics**  
- Boggards start hidden behind the fallen pine, using **Stealth DC 16**.  
- They attack when half the party crosses the log, using **Tongue** to pull targets toward the water.  
- The giant frog joins after the first round, focusing on isolated PCs.

---

**Victory Conditions**  
- Ambushers routed or captured; clues on the bodies point upriver to the wreck site.

# Wrecked Barge Stabilization
## Victory Point Subsystem (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Subsystem== ==Multiple Point== ==Kyonin River==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
A spring flood has left a merchant barge jammed between two boulders mid-river. Its hull groans under the current while ropes strain and cargo shifts dangerously. The heroes must work together to secure the vessel before it breaks apart and is lost to the river.

---

**Subsystem Type**  
*Accumulating Victory Points vs. Environmental Collapse Points*  
- **Success Threshold:** 8 Victory Points  
- **Collapse Threshold:** 3 Collapse Points  
- **Scale:** Long Encounter (7–10 VP End Point)  
- **Typical Duration:** 3–5 rounds of skill checks (≈ 1 minute per round)

---

**Degrees of Success (Accumulating Rolls)**  
- **Critical Success:** +2 Victory Points  
- **Success:** +1 Victory Point  
- **Failure:** +0 Victory Points and +1 Collapse Point, 1d6 Bludgeoning DC 15 Basic Reflex
- **Critical Failure:** –1 Victory Point and +1 Collapse Point , 1d6 Bludgeoning DC 15 Basic Reflex

The challenge ends when either threshold is reached.

---

**Primary Skills (DC 15)**  
- **Athletics:** Pull lines, brace timbers, haul cargo clear of water.  
- **Crafting:** Patch leaks, tighten lashings, reinforce hull seams.  
- **Nature:** Judge current strength, pick optimal anchor angles.  
- **Survival:** Secure footing, guide the group’s movement safely.

**Secondary Skills (DC 13, each once per scene)**  
- **Perception:** Spot structural weaknesses; grants +1 Victory Point on next PC success.  
- **Aid:** Example: Arcana- Use minor magic to harden bindings or calm water; +1 circumstance bonus to next Crafting check.  
- **Diplomacy:** ==Fortune== Coordinate efforts, boosting morale; allows one reroll for another PC this round.  

---

**Consequences**  
- Each Collapse Point represents environmental degradation — snapping ropes, shifting cargo, flooding hull.  
- Narrative descriptions should escalate tension as Collapse Points accumulate.

---

**Outcomes**  
- **Success (8 VP before 3 Collapse Points):** The barge is secured and partially unloaded. The party earns full 80 XP.  
- **Partial Success (≥4 VP but 3 Collapse Points reached):** Half the cargo is lost; full XP awarded but reduced gold payout per GM discretion.  
- **Failure (<4 VP when 3 Collapse Points reached):** The barge breaks free and drifts away; party earns half XP (40).

# Causeway Standoff
## Encounter 2 (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Kyonin Causeway==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
The heroes follow the river north until they reach a crumbling elven causeway spanning the Kyonic River. Shattered pillars and broken stones rise above the rapids. Atop the ruin, boggard raiders are stripping cargo from the recovered barge and holding its surviving crew hostage.

Three surviving **Riverspire deckhands**—bruised and bound—are forced to assist the raiders, moving crates toward the far side of the bridge. Their lives hang in the balance once the fighting starts.

---

**Creatures**  
- **[Boggard Scout](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Scout&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4000)** ×1 
- **[Boggard Guard](obsidian://opengate?title=Boggard%20Guard&url=https%3A%2F%2F2e.aonprd.com%2FMonsters.aspx%3FID%3D4006)** ×2  
*(Adjust numbers as needed to maintain ~80 XP total.)*

---

**Terrain**  
- **Ancient Elven Causeway:** 10 ft above rushing water; falling deals **1d6 bludgeoning** and sweeps a creature 20 ft downstream each round until they Swim DC 14 to recover.  
- **Broken Parapets:** Provide standard cover.  
- **Oil-Slick Crates:** Several leaking barrels create flammable hazards (see *Oil Fire Slick* hazard).  

---

**Tactics**  
- The leader positions the crew as living shields, forcing the PCs to choose between cautious rescue or direct assault.  Crew provides "Lesser Cover" for the Boggards. Critical miss with a range weapon strikes a crew member.  
- Boggard guards attempt to shove PCs toward the river; the leader focuses on holding hostages or setting the oil aflame if losing.

---

**Rescuing the Crew**  
- **Athletics DC 14** to cut bonds quickly.  
- **Diplomacy DC 15** to give freed crew simple commands

---

**Victory Conditions**  
- Defeat or rout the raiders (XP as normal).  
- Recover remaining cargo to complete the contract from Riverspire.

---

**Outcome**  
- **Victory:** Crew rescued, raiders defeated, cargo secured. The party earns full 80 XP.  
- **Partial:** Cargo burns; full XP, Gold reward reduced by half.  
- **Failure:** Raiders escape with captives; half XP (40).

# Oil Fire Slick
## Complex Hazard -1

==Hazard== ==Complex== ==Environmental== ==Fire==

**Stealth** +7 (trained) to notice the slicks before ignition; **Description** Leaking lamp oil has spread across the broken causeway stones and into shallow eddies below. Any spark or flame can ignite the film, sending fire racing across the surface.

---

**Disable** `[one-action]` **Crafting, Athletics, or Arcana DC 15** to smother, divert, or magically dampen a 5-foot patch of oil. Each successful check removes that section from the hazard’s area. The hazard ends once all patches are disabled or burn out.

**AC** 15 **Fort +5 Ref +8** **Hardness** 5 **HP** 20 (BT 10) per 5-foot patch

**Immunities** Critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

---

**Trigger**  
Any open flame, lightning effect, or critical hit that produces sparks within 10 feet of the slick ignites a patch.

---

**Routine** (1 action)  
At the start of each round after ignition, flames spread 5 feet in one direction (GM’s choice or by wind/current).  
Creatures in or adjacent to a burning patch take **1d4 fire damage (Basic Reflex DC 16)**.  
A creature that fails this save also catches **persistent 1 fire**.  
The slick burns for **2 rounds** after ignition, then gutters out unless reignited by another patch.

---

**Reset**  
The oil burns out naturally after 2 rounds of inactivity or if fully smothered.  
If new oil leaks from barrels, the hazard reforms in 10 minutes.

---

**Notes**  
- Treat each patch as a 5-foot hazard space; 4–6 patches typically cover the causeway.
