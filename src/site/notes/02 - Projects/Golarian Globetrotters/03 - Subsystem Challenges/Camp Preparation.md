# Camp Preparation — Site Defense Readiness
## Short Subsystem

==Subsystem== ==Camp== ==Preparation==

**Party Level** — Variable **XP** — None **Treasure** — None

---

**Scenario**  
Before nightfall, the party prepares a defensible camp near the prospective claim. This subsystem represents how well they secure the area, organize rest, and stabilize morale. It does **not** change how likely an encounter is; it determines how ready the party is if something attacks during the night.

---

**Subsystem Type & Structure**  

This is a lightweight preparation subsystem that modifies the next night encounter at this camp.

- **Prep Window:** Up to 3 hours before sleep.  
- Each PC may take **one meaningful prep action**.  
- Each action is **one Skill Check** tied to a prep category.  
- **Prep Points:** Success and critical success grant Prep Points in that category.  
- **Cap:** Maximum of 6 total Prep Points across all categories to avoid runaway stacking.

**Degrees of success for individual prep checks:**

- **Critical Success:** Gain **2 Prep Points** in the chosen category.  
- **Success:** Gain **1 Prep Point** in the chosen category.  
- **Failure:** No effect.  
- **Critical Failure:** Minor mishap (such as 1d6 damage or starting the encounter **Frightened 1**, GM’s choice based on fiction).

---

**Degrees of Success**  

The subsystem itself does not have a pass/fail VP total; instead, the total Prep Points across categories determine modifiers applied if a night encounter occurs.

- Higher total points → stronger opening position, better initiative, more durability.  
- Poor prep or multiple critical failures → weaker start, more exposed camp.

---

**Primary Skills (Typical DC 15)**  

The exact skill depends on the chosen prep category:

- **Fortify the Camp (Physical Defense):**  
  - Crafting, Athletics, Survival, Engineering Lore, Dwarven Lore  
  - Building barricades, trenches, bracing stone, shaping chokepoints.

- **Establish Watch & Early Warning (Awareness):**  
  - Perception, Survival, Stealth  
  - Setting lookouts, noise traps, alarm lines, and sentry patterns.

- **Secure Living Space (Morale & Stability):**  
  - Survival, Medicine, Diplomacy, Performance  
  - Organizing shelters, reinforcing rest, tending stress and minor wounds.

- **Appease / Respect the Wild (Optional, Thematic):**  
  - Nature, Religion, Diplomacy, Occultism  
  - Offerings, ritual gestures, symbolic respect to the land and its spirits.  
  - Recommended DC 17 (fey and spirits are fickle).

---

**Secondary Skills (List limitations if any, DC __)**  

None defined. All meaningful contributions use the primary skills above in the context of a chosen prep category.

---

**Consequences / Setbacks**  

Prep failures represent wasted effort, awkward layout, or frayed nerves. Critical failures represent small injuries, embarrassment, or stress that may carry into the encounter (GM chooses an appropriate minor penalty: 1d6 damage, Frightened 1, or similar).

Prep does **not** prevent an encounter from happening. Instead, it determines:

- Who is ready and in position  
- Who has better initiative  
- Who has better cover or defense  
- Whether the enemy hesitates or hits immediately

---

**Outcomes**  

If **no night encounter occurs**, the subsystem simply reinforces that the party took their work seriously; no mechanical effect is required.

If a **night encounter occurs at this camp**, apply:

- **Fortify the Camp (Fortification Points)**  
  - Each Fortification Point: one PC gains **+1 AC vs their first attack**.  
  - At **3+ Fortification Points:** the camp clearly has established cover; PCs may claim Cover from built defenses without argument.

- **Establish Watch & Early Warning (Awareness Points)**  
  - Each Awareness Point: assign **+1 Initiative** to a PC.  
  - At **2+ Awareness Points:** the party is **not Flat-Footed** at the start of the encounter.  
  - At **3+ Awareness Points:** they detect the threat moments earlier in narration, reinforcing their readiness.

- **Secure Living Space (Morale Points)**  
  - Each Morale Point: all PCs gain **1 Temporary HP** at the start of the encounter.  
  - At **2+ Morale Points:** +1 bonus to the **first Will save** each PC attempts in the encounter.  
  - At **3+ Morale Points:** remove **Fatigued** conditions gained from the site work earlier that day.

- **Appease / Respect the Wild (Ritual Prep)**  
  - On **Success (DC 17):** the first hostile attack against the party deals **–1 damage**.  
  - On **Critical Success:** the enemy hesitates; they **lose their first Reaction** in round 1.  
  - On **Critical Failure:** the attempt offends the land or its spirits; the enemy gains **+1 to their first attack roll**.

These effects apply only to the **next night encounter at this camp** and do not persist beyond it.
