# Wrecked Barge Stabilization
## Victory Point Subsystem (Moderate)

==Encounter== ==Moderate== ==Subsystem== ==Multiple Point== ==Kyonin River==

**Party Level** 1 **XP Budget** 80 **Treasure (Gold Only)** 18 gp

---

**Scenario**  
A spring flood has left a merchant barge jammed between two boulders mid-river. Its hull groans under the current while ropes strain and cargo shifts dangerously. The heroes must work together to secure the vessel before it breaks apart and is lost to the river.

---

**Subsystem Type**  
*Accumulating Victory Points vs. Environmental Collapse Points*  
- **Success Threshold:** 8 Victory Points  
- **Collapse Threshold:** 3 Collapse Points  
- **Scale:** Long Encounter (7–10 VP End Point)  
- **Typical Duration:** 3–5 rounds of skill checks (≈ 1 minute per round)

---

**Degrees of Success (Accumulating Rolls)**  
- **Critical Success:** +2 Victory Points  
- **Success:** +1 Victory Point  
- **Failure:** +0 Victory Points and +1 Collapse Point, 1d6 Bludgeoning DC 15 Basic Reflex
- **Critical Failure:** –1 Victory Point and +1 Collapse Point , 1d6 Bludgeoning DC 15 Basic Reflex

The challenge ends when either threshold is reached.

---

**Primary Skills (DC 15)**  
- **Athletics:** Pull lines, brace timbers, haul cargo clear of water.  
- **Crafting:** Patch leaks, tighten lashings, reinforce hull seams.  
- **Nature:** Judge current strength, pick optimal anchor angles.  
- **Survival:** Secure footing, guide the group’s movement safely.

**Secondary Skills (DC 13, each once per scene)**  
- **Perception:** Spot structural weaknesses; grants +1 Victory Point on next PC success.  
- **Aid:** Example: Arcana- Use minor magic to harden bindings or calm water; +1 circumstance bonus to next Crafting check.  
- **Diplomacy:** ==Fortune== Coordinate efforts, boosting morale; allows one reroll for another PC this round.  

---

**Consequences**  
- Each Collapse Point represents environmental degradation — snapping ropes, shifting cargo, flooding hull.  
- Narrative descriptions should escalate tension as Collapse Points accumulate.

---

**Outcomes**  
- **Success (8 VP before 3 Collapse Points):** The barge is secured and partially unloaded. The party earns full 80 XP.  
- **Partial Success (≥4 VP but 3 Collapse Points reached):** Half the cargo is lost; full XP awarded but reduced gold payout per GM discretion.  
- **Failure (<4 VP when 3 Collapse Points reached):** The barge breaks free and drifts away; party earns half XP (40).
